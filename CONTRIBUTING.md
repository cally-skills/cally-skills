# Contributing to Cally Skills

Thank you for helping improve the Alpha. Contributions are reviewed under the repository's public/private and licensing boundaries.

## Welcome in Alpha

- compatibility fixes;
- Codex, Muse Code, Qwen Code, and other adapters;
- documentation corrections;
- installation and validation fixes;
- proposed synthetic test cases;
- reproducible bug reports.

Keep proposed cases fictional, minimal, and free of personal or client information. Do not submit private consultation transcripts, credentials, or third-party confidential material.

## Maintainer-only methodology

Only maintainers may modify or approve changes to:

- judgment thresholds;
- risk-escalation rules;
- directional-judgment boundaries;
- proprietary Cally methodology;
- branded Cally output logic;
- private methodology or evaluation artifacts.

External contributors may report a behavior problem or propose a test demonstrating it. A maintainer decides whether and how the underlying methodology changes.

## Pull request expectations

- Explain the observed problem and the scope of the change.
- Keep platform-specific behavior in adapters instead of forking the canonical Skill.
- Add or update a focused public regression test when appropriate.
- Do not include generated Skill copies, local paths, logs, secrets, or private eval output.
- Do not weaken safety boundaries or expand the Skill into an unapproved domain.

Submitting a contribution does not override the repository's licensing terms. During Alpha, proposed contributions may be reviewed case by case. Core Cally methodology remains maintainer-only.
